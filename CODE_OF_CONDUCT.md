# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be considerate, specific, and constructive. Disagreement about designs and code
is welcome; harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, and the
publication of another person's private information are not.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the project owner through GitHub.
Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently ban
participants whose conduct harms the community. Reports will be handled as
confidentially as practical.

This compact policy follows the intent of the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
